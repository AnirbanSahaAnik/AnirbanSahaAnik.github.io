Ph.D. Student · NLP & Large Language Models

Anirban Saha Anik.

University of North Texas · Denton, Texas

I study how language models use evidence, adapt to readers, and communicate reliably in health and crisis settings. My work connects evidence-grounded generation, human-adaptive responses, and rigorous evaluation to make AI support more factual, understandable, useful, and safe.

I combine retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge graphs, multi-agent systems, controllable text generation, and human evaluation, with a focus on health misinformation and crisis communication.

Working with Dr. Lingzi Hong in the Human-Centered Computing Lab.

Grounding & knowledge

Evidence-grounded language models ↗

I build systems that use retrieval, knowledge graphs, and collaborating agents to find authoritative evidence. I focus on responses that are factual, traceable, and robust.

People & interaction

Adaptive human-AI interaction ↗

I study when AI should ask, answer, adapt, or collaborate. Controllable generation, health literacy, and human feedback guide explanations that fit the reader while preserving factual meaning.

Measurement & reliability

Evaluation for high-stakes NLP ↗

I combine benchmarks, automated metrics, and human studies to evaluate factuality, consistency, audience alignment, and actionability. I also test whether responses reflect the right context, place, and time.
