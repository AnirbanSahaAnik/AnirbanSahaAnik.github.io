01 / About

I work at the intersection of language technology and human needs.

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Engineering at the University of North Texas, advised by Dr. Lingzi Hong, and a Graduate Research Assistant in the Human-Centered Computing Lab.

My work spans retrieval-augmented generation, knowledge graphs, multi-agent systems, controllable generation, and LLM evaluation, with a focus on health misinformation and crisis response.
