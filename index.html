01 / Research focus

From capable language models to dependable ones.

I study how language models can communicate reliable information in high-stakes settings. Working with Dr. Lingzi Hong in the Human-Centered Computing Lab.

I connect evidence-grounded generation, human-adaptive responses, and rigorous evaluation to produce safer AI support for health misinformation and crisis communication.

Grounding & knowledge

Evidence-grounded language models

I build retrieval, knowledge-graph, and multi-agent methods that connect model outputs to authoritative evidence—making high-stakes responses more factual, traceable, and robust.

Explore this research area →
People & interaction

Adaptive human–AI interaction

I study when an AI should ask, answer, adapt, or collaborate—using controllable generation, literacy-aware methods, and human feedback to fit the reader and the moment.

Explore this research area →
Measurement & reliability

Evaluation for high-stakes NLP

I design benchmarks, metrics, and human studies that test factuality, consistency, audience alignment, actionability, and situational awareness in health and crisis communication.
