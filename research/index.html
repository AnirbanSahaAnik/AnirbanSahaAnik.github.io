Research

Language systems grounded in evidence and designed around people.

My research connects reliable knowledge use, audience adaptation, and rigorous evaluation in high-stakes communication.

01

Knowledge-grounded generation

I build retrieval-augmented generation and knowledge-graph methods that connect LLM outputs to trusted evidence. My work studies multi-agent orchestration, static and dynamic retrieval, evidence selection, and structured biomedical knowledge.

  • RAG
  • Knowledge Graphs
  • Evidence Grounding
  • Multi-Agent LLMs

02

Controllable & personalized generation

I design generation methods that adapt explanations to readers' health literacy, knowledge, and information needs while preserving factual accuracy. This includes literacy-controlled generation and systems that learn when clarification is worth asking for.

  • Controllable Generation
  • Personalization
  • Health Literacy
  • Reinforcement Learning

03

Evaluation & benchmarking

I develop benchmarks, metrics, and human-evaluation protocols for factuality, faithfulness, consistency, and audience alignment. The goal is to measure whether a model is not only fluent, but reliable and useful to the person reading it.

  • LLM Evaluation
  • Benchmarks
  • Factuality
  • Human Evaluation

04

Health & crisis communication

I apply these methods in settings where communication quality matters: evidence-based responses to health misinformation and timely, consistent responses during crises. These domains motivate the technical questions across my research.

  • Health Misinformation
  • Crisis Informatics
  • Responsible AI
  • Human-Centered NLP
