Research / Ph.D. research agenda

Building trustworthy language systems.

My research connects reliable evidence, adaptive human–AI interaction, and rigorous evaluation to help language models communicate responsibly in health and crisis settings.

01 / RESEARCH AREAS

Three connected themes.

From getting the right evidence to communicating it with the right level of detail.

01

Evidence-grounded language models

Building language systems that retrieve reliable evidence and synthesize answers with traceable support. I study knowledge graphs, retrieval-augmented generation, collaborating agents, and evidence selection for health communication.

Selected related papers

02

Adaptive human–AI interaction

Understanding when an AI system should ask a clarifying question, deliver a direct answer, or change the level of explanation. This line of work addresses health literacy, audience-specific communication, and controllable text generation.

Selected related papers

EMNLP 2025 · Speaking at the Right Level↗EMNLP 2026 · Ask or Answer↗
03

Evaluation for high-stakes NLP

Testing whether model outputs are factually correct, appropriately contextualized, useful to readers, and grounded in the right place and time. The goal is to expose failures that surface-level fluency or generic metrics can conceal.
