01 / MOTIVATION

Why this work matters

When crisis information needs are urgent, generated answers should be both informative and actionable. Different models can contribute different strengths.

A fusion approach combining multiple language models and retrieval for timely and actionable crisis communication.

02 / METHODOLOGY

Research approach

The earlier ISCRAM study explores retrieval-supported dynamic fusion to combine complementary language models for crisis communication.

03 / CONTRIBUTIONS

Main contribution

Comparing prompting, RAG, and dynamic fusion of LLMs for crisis information needs.
