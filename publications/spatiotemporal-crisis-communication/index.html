Can LLMs Reason Over Where and When? Benchmarking Spatiotemporal Synthesis in Crisis Response

Sawyer Alston, Yashaswini Sreenivasa Shetty, Sreeja Kanuganti, Anirban Saha Anik, Xiaoying Song, Lingzi Hong

Testing whether language models ground crisis responses in information from the right place and time.

In this article

MotivationMethodologyContributionsPublication

01 / MOTIVATION

Why this work matters

A crisis response may sound plausible while using information from the wrong location or an outdated moment.

The research examines whether generated answers rely on semantically relevant, geographically aligned and temporally appropriate evidence.

02 / METHODOLOGY

Benchmarking evidence use

  1. 01

    Start with a crisis need

    Represent what information a user needs.

  2. 02

    Audit evidence alignment

    Examine semantic relevance, spatial grounding and temporal grounding.

  3. 03

    Evaluate grounded synthesis

    Study whether combined evidence supports an appropriate response.

03 / CONTRIBUTIONS

What the benchmark contributes

Diagnostic evaluation of spatiotemporal grounding in crisis communications, with controlled evidence chains and crisis-need queries.

01

128 diagnostic queries

Questions designed to expose grounding failures.

02

3 grounding dimensions

Semantic, spatial and temporal alignment.
