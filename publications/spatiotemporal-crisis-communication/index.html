ST-Crisis: A Diagnostic Benchmark for Spatiotemporal Grounding in Crisis Communication

A diagnostic benchmark for testing whether crisis-response systems use evidence from the right place and time.

Dataset ↗

The problem

Why this work matters

A crisis response can sound plausible while relying on information from the wrong location or an outdated moment. Existing evaluations do not adequately diagnose whether generated answers are grounded in semantically, spatially, and temporally appropriate evidence.
