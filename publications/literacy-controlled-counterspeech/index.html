01 / MOTIVATION

Why this work matters

One explanation may be too technical for some readers and too simplified for others. Controlled-Literacy makes readability an explicit design objective, retrieving level-appropriate knowledge and training generation with readability and user-preference feedback.

Research objective

Adapting evidence-grounded counterspeech to the target reader's health literacy through retrieval and reinforcement learning.

02 / METHOD

Methodology

  1. 01

    Collect evidence from COVID-19 public-health, educational, and scientific sources.

  2. 02

    Define low, medium, and high health-literacy targets with distinct readability ranges.

  3. 03

    Combine keyword and semantic retrieval, filtering evidence by readability and preference.

  4. 04

    Use GRPO to optimize a LoRA-adapted model with equally weighted readability and simulated reader preference rewards.

04 / DISCUSSION

Discussion and analysis

Politeness and factual accuracy improve with Controlled-Literacy for all three models. Readability distance improves for LLaMA-8B and LLaMA-1B but worsens for Qwen-7B compared with the other approaches. The paper therefore supports audience alignment with meaningful model-dependent differences.
