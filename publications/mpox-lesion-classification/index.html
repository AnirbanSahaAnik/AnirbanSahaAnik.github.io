01 / MOTIVATION

Why this work matters

Visually similar skin conditions can be difficult to differentiate in images; model explanations can help examine what a classifier attends to.

An interpretable attention-guided model for distinguishing MPox from visually similar skin conditions.

02 / METHODOLOGY

Research approach

The proposed attention-guided fusion network uses Grad-CAM visualization to interpret MPox skin-lesion classifications.

03 / CONTRIBUTIONS

Main contribution

Hybrid attention-guided image classification with Grad-CAM explanations for MPox skin lesions.
