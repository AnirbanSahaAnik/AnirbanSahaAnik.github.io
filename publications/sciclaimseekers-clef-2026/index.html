01 / MOTIVATION

Why this work matters

Scientific evidence can be difficult to retrieve from the informal wording of social-media claims. The work investigates how candidate retrieval and language-model reranking can prioritize better scientific sources.

A scientific-source retrieval system that combines initial retrieval with LLM-based reranking for social-media claims.

02 / METHODOLOGY

Research approach

Candidate scientific documents are retrieved broadly, then an LLM-based reranker selects sources that more closely support checking the claim.

03 / CONTRIBUTIONS

Main contribution

Scientific evidence retrieval strengthened by LLM reranking.
