01 / MOTIVATION

Why this work matters

Numerical claims can be deceptively difficult to verify, since small differences in quantities, units and context can change the verdict.

A fine-tuned LLM approach for numerical claim verification that placed second in the shared task.

02 / METHODOLOGY

Research approach

The shared-task system uses fine-tuned language models to verify numerical statements; it ranked second in its CheckThat! task.

03 / CONTRIBUTIONS

Main contribution

Fine-tuned LLMs for verification of numerical claims.

  • 2nd place in CheckThat! Lab 2026 Task 2
