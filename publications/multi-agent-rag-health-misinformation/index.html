01 / MOTIVATION

Why this work matters

Static medical references can provide dependable information but miss emerging developments. Dynamic search adds timely context but introduces varying source quality. The paper asks how agents can coordinate these evidence types and then refine the response for factuality, relevance, informativeness, and politeness.

Research objective

Combining curated medical resources, dynamic web evidence, and specialized agents to generate grounded health-misinformation corrections.

02 / METHOD

Methodology

  1. 01

    Retrieve evidence from a curated health knowledge base with semantic and keyword matching.

  2. 02

    Retrieve dynamic web information and filter weaker evidence.

  3. 03

    Rank and summarize the top ten pieces of evidence using BM25 and embeddings.

  4. 04

    Generate grounded counterspeech and refine it for clarity and respectful communication.

04 / DISCUSSION

Discussion and analysis

The proposed framework is strongest on politeness, informativeness, and factual accuracy, and ties direct prompting on relevance. Ablations highlight trade-offs: removing refinement lowers politeness but may improve informativeness or factual accuracy in this experiment.
