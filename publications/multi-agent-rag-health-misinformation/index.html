Multi-Agent Retrieval-Augmented Framework for Evidence-Based Counterspeech Against Health Misinformation

Anirban Saha Anik, Xiaoying Song, Elliott Wang, Bryan Wang, Bengisu Yarimbas, and Lingzi Hong

A coordinated system of specialized agents that retrieves, synthesizes, generates, and refines evidence-based counterspeech.

Paper ↗Dataset ↗Poster ↗BibTeX ↗

The problem

Why this work matters

Conventional RAG systems often depend on a narrow evidence source and provide limited control over response quality. For health misinformation, a useful correction must be current, factual, relevant, and respectful at the same time.

Methodology

How the approach works

Contributions

What this work contributes

  1. 01

    A modular multi-agent architecture for evidence retrieval, summarization, response generation, and refinement.

  2. 02

    Joint use of curated medical guidance and real-time web evidence for both authority and timeliness.

  3. 03

    A public Reddit health-misinformation dataset covering COVID-19, influenza, and HIV claims.

  4. 04

    Ablation and human-preference studies that isolate the value of each agent and evidence path.
